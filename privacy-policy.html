Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Overview

Auto Refresh is a browser extension that automatically reloads the current tab on a user-defined interval. Your privacy is important to us, and this extension is designed with a privacy-first approach.

Data Collection

Auto Refresh does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No browsing history or page content is accessed or recorded
  • No cookies or tracking mechanisms are used
  • No data is sent to any external server
  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind is implemented

Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • alarms — Used for a periodic 30-second keepalive that lets the background service worker re-arm pending refresh timers if Chrome suspends it
  • storage — Persists per-tab refresh interval and the next-fire timestamp in chrome.storage.local on your device
  • tabs — Calls chrome.tabs.reload() on the tab you started refresh on, and detects when that tab is closed so the timer can be cleared

The extension does not request <all_urls> and does not inject any content scripts into web pages.

Local Storage

Two pieces of local-only state are stored:

  • Per-tab refresh interval and next-fire timestamp (in chrome.storage.local), used to keep timers running across popup close and service worker suspension
  • Theme preference (light/dark mode), saved in the popup’s localStorage

Neither leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
